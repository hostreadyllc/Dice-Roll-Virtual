Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how Dice Roller (“the app”), published by Host Ready LLC, handles information. In short: the app does not collect or share your personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Your settings — selected theme, number of dice, sound, vibration, shake and 3D preferences, and your roll history — are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Device features

  • Motion sensor (accelerometer): used only to detect when you shake your phone to roll the dice. Motion data is processed on your device and is not stored or transmitted.
  • Vibration and sound: used for roll feedback. You can turn both off in the app’s settings.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

In-app purchases

Premium themes may be offered as a one-time in-app purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details.

To confirm purchases and let you restore them on another device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from the store. This information is not linked to your name or contact details and is used only to provide your purchase.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone, because we do not collect it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data deletion

All app data lives on your device. To delete it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. If you have any questions about your data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
