Frequently asked questions

How do I roll?
Tap ROLL, tap the table, or shake your phone. When you shake, the dice keep rattling until you stop.

How do I keep a die between rolls?
Tap the die to hold it — a ring appears under it. Tap it again to release it.

Shaking doesn’t roll the dice.
Open settings (🎨) and check that Shake phone to roll is on. Shake a little more firmly; gentle movements are ignored so the dice don’t roll by accident.

There’s no sound or vibration.
Check that Sound effects and Vibration are on in settings, that your phone isn’t on silent, and that vibration is enabled in your phone’s settings.

The dice look flat, not 3D.
If your phone had trouble showing 3D dice, the app switches to flat dice automatically so it keeps working. You can try turning 3D dice back on in settings.

I bought premium themes. How do I get them on a new phone?
Open settings (🎨) and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used to buy.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Google Play (request a refund) or the Apple App Store (report a problem).
