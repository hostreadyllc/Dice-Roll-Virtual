# Privacy Policy — Dice Roller

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Dice Roller ("the app") is published by Host Ready LLC.

## Information we collect

The app does **not** collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your settings (selected theme, number of dice, sound and vibration preferences) are saved **only on your device**.

## In-app purchases

If you buy premium themes, payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

We use **RevenueCat** to confirm purchases and restore them on your other devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts from the store. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Device features

- **Motion sensor**: used only to detect a shake to roll the dice. Motion data is not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Vibration and sound**: used for roll feedback and can be turned off in Settings.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
